# Valise

**A packing muse that plans your trip wardrobe, then roasts your overpacking to one decimal place.**

Live: https://sonum-library.github.io/valise/

---

Valise is not a packing checklist. A checklist makes you do all the
thinking. Valise decides.

Tell it what kind of trip you are taking, and it interviews you, checks
the real forecast for your destination, learns how you pack, builds a
capsule wardrobe and a tailored list, catches what you have forgotten,
plans your outfits, clocks your duplicates, and tells you whether your
case is actually going to close.

It also has opinions.

## What it does

- **Trip types.** Beach, city break, business, wedding, adventure, ski,
  visiting family, romantic, resort and spa, camping, all-inclusive, or
  "no idea, just help me". Each sets a smart baseline and shifts the
  whole palette.
- **Live weather.** Enter a destination and it pulls the real forecast,
  sets the climate, and adds rain gear when rain is likely. It also
  reads the destination country's plug type and packs the right adapter.
- **Packing personality.** Minimalist, sensible, overpacker, or feral.
  Quantities genuinely change. Feral includes an emotional-support
  cardigan.
- **A few honest questions.** "Actually going to the gym? Be honest."
  Say no and the gym clothes disappear.
- **Non-negotiables.** Add your own rules once and it remembers them for
  every future trip.
- **The forgetting radar.** Swimwear but no cover-up, rain but nothing
  waterproof, the wedding dress but no heels.
- **Pieces and outfits.** Add your real clothes and it builds a
  day-by-day outfit plan and flags your duplicates.
- **The suitcase reality check.** Pick your bags and it estimates how
  full and how heavy you are, then helps you talk it back down.

## How it is built

A single self-contained HTML file. No build step, no framework, no
backend, no accounts, no tracking. Everything is saved locally in your
own browser. Hosted free on GitHub Pages.

Weather and geocoding come from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/).
Typefaces are served by Google Fonts.

Packing estimates are approximations. Check your airline's baggage
rules, darling.
